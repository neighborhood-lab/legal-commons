ARTICLE VII: DISSOLUTION

7.1 Events of Dissolution. The Company shall dissolve upon the earliest to occur of:

(a) The written agreement of Members holding a majority of the ownership interests;
(b) The entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under the laws of the State of {{state}};
(c) Any event which makes it unlawful for the business of the Company to be carried on.

7.2 Winding Up. Upon dissolution, the Company shall be wound up and its assets distributed in the following order:

(a) To creditors, including Members who are creditors, in satisfaction of liabilities of the Company;
(b) To the Members in proportion to their positive capital account balances.
