IMPORTANT DELAWARE FILING INFORMATION:

1. This Certificate must be filed with the Delaware Secretary of State, Division of Corporations along with the required filing fee.
2. The registered agent must be located in Delaware and can be an individual resident of Delaware or a business entity authorized to do business in Delaware.
3. File IRS Form 2553 if electing S-Corporation tax treatment.
4. Obtain an Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS.
5. Delaware LLCs must pay an annual franchise tax of $300 to the Delaware Division of Corporations.
6. No publication requirement in Delaware.
7. Delaware offers strong legal protections and a well-established body of business law.
8. An LLC Agreement should be adopted, though Delaware does not require it to be filed with the state.
9. The company name must contain the words "Limited Liability Company" or the abbreviation "L.L.C." or "LLC."
